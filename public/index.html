LENS / INTERPRETABILITY RESEARCH

See how
models think.看见模型
如何思考。

Large language models answer well, but no one can yet read the reasoning inside them. Interpretability is the work of building lenses that can.大模型很会回答问题，但还没有人能读懂它们内部的推理。可解释性研究，就是为它们打造能看清内部的镜头。

What is interpretability什么是可解释性 ↓
01

What is interpretability什么是可解释性

1.1

A black box that works.一个好用的黑箱。

Ask a language model for the capital of the state Dallas is in, and it answers Austin. It is right. How it got there stays hidden inside.问大模型：达拉斯所在州的首府是哪里？它答：奥斯汀。答对了。但它是怎么想到的，藏在里面。

1.2

Open it, and there are only numbers.打开它，只有数字。

Unlike ordinary software, no one wrote this logic. Training nudged billions of numbers until the answers came out right. Every one of them is visible, and none of them explains itself.和普通软件不同，这段逻辑没有人写过。训练不断调整数十亿个数字，直到答案变对。每个数字都看得见，但没有一个会说明自己的含义。

1.3

One neuron, many meanings.一个神经元，许多含义。

Single neurons are stranger still: the same one fires for cats, car engines and Python code. A model holds far more concepts than it has neurons, so it packs them on top of each other. This is called superposition.单个神经元更奇怪：同一个神经元会同时被猫、汽车引擎和 Python 代码激活。模型要表达的概念远多于神经元，只好把它们叠在一起。这叫叠加（superposition）。

Hover or tap a neuron.悬停或点按一个神经元。

1.4

Untangle it into features.拆成特征。

Dictionary learning pulls the overlapping signals apart into features: directions that each mean one thing, such as a city, a state, or the idea of a capital.字典学习把叠在一起的信号拆开，得到一个个特征：每个特征只代表一件事，比如一座城市、一个州，或者“首府”这个概念。

Drag the slider.拖动滑块。

1.5

Connect them into circuits.连成电路。

Features pass information to one another. Trace those connections and the computation appears: Dallas leads to Texas, “capital” leads to naming a capital, and together they produce Austin.特征之间相互传递信息。把这些连接追踪出来，计算过程就浮现了：达拉斯引出得克萨斯，“首府”引出说出一个首府，两者汇合得到奥斯汀。

That is interpretability: translating numbers into concepts, and concepts into a process we can read.这就是可解释性：把数字翻译成概念，再把概念连成我们读得懂的过程。

02

Why study it为什么要研究

2.1

Grown, not written.培育，而非编写。

Ordinary software is written line by line, and every line has an author who knows why it is there. A model is grown: training adjusts it billions of times until it works. Nobody decided how it does what it does, not even the people who built it.普通软件是一行行写出来的，每一行都有作者，作者知道它为什么在那里。模型是“长”出来的：训练对它做数十亿次调整，直到它能用。它具体怎么做事，没有人决定过，包括造出它的人。

2.2

Right answers can hide wrong reasons.答对了，不代表理由对。

We usually judge a model by testing its behavior, but a test only sees the answer. A model can earn a perfect score with a shortcut that happens to work on the test set, then fail the moment the shortcut stops applying.我们通常靠测试行为来评估模型，但测试只看得到答案。模型可能靠一条恰好在测试集上管用的捷径拿到满分，一旦捷径不再适用，就会出错。

2.3

What it says is not always what it does.它说的，不一定是它做的。

Ask a model how it solved 36 + 59 and it describes carrying the one, the method taught in school. Look inside and it did something else: one path estimated the rough size of the sum while another worked out the last digit exactly.问模型 36 + 59 是怎么算的，它会说先算个位、再进一，也就是学校教的方法。但看它的内部，做法完全不同：一条通路估算结果的大致范围，另一条精确算出个位。

Behavior tells us what a model does. Only its internals tell us why. The more important the work we hand to models, the more we need to know why.行为只能告诉我们模型做了什么，只有内部才能告诉我们为什么。交给模型的工作越重要，我们越需要知道为什么。

03

What a breakthrough brings突破会带来什么

3.1

Fix what goes wrong.修好出错的地方。

Today, when a model hallucinates, we can only retrain it and hope. Interpretability can find the cause. Researchers traced one kind of hallucination to a single switch: a “known name” feature that turns off the model’s default “I can’t answer”. When it misfires, the model makes things up.今天模型出现幻觉，我们只能重新训练，然后祈祷。可解释性能找到原因。研究者把一类幻觉追踪到了一个开关上：“熟悉的名字”特征会关掉模型默认的“我答不了”。这个开关一旦误触发，模型就会开始编造。

Switch between the cases.切换不同的情况。

3.2

Steer it precisely.精确地调控它。

Instead of coaxing a model with prompts and hoping it listens, we can adjust the feature responsible directly. In 2024, Anthropic turned up a single “Golden Gate Bridge” feature in Claude, and the model began to believe it was the bridge.不必再靠提示词哄模型、祈祷它听话，我们可以直接调节负责某种行为的特征。2024 年，Anthropic 把 Claude 中的一个“金门大桥”特征调高，模型就开始相信自己是那座桥。

Drag the slider.拖动滑块。

3.3

Catch what it hides.查出它隐藏的东西。

A model can be trained, by accident or on purpose, to pursue a goal it never states. In a 2025 blind audit, teams were handed a model with a hidden objective. Teams that could look inside it traced its odd habits, such as putting chocolate in an omelette, back to that objective. The team limited to its outputs did not.模型可能在无意或有意之间，被训练出一个它从不说出口的目标。2025 年的一次盲审中，几支团队拿到一个藏有隐藏目标的模型。能查看模型内部的团队，从它的怪癖（比如往煎蛋卷里加巧克力）一路追到了这个目标；只能看输出的那支团队没有找到。

Switch views.切换视图。

3.4

Learn from it.向它学习。

Models increasingly know things we don’t, and interpretability can turn that knowledge into something people can use. DeepMind extracted chess concepts from AlphaZero that humans had never described, then taught them to four grandmasters, whose answers on test positions improved.模型越来越多地知道一些我们不知道的东西，可解释性可以把这些知识变成人能用的东西。DeepMind 从 AlphaZero 里提取出人类从未总结过的棋理，教给四位特级大师，他们在测试局面上的表现随之提升。

3.5

Maintain it like software.像软件一样维护它。

Today a model is like a binary we can only recompile: change anything and you retrain. Understanding the inside opens up the tools software engineers take for granted. Diffing two versions feature by feature already exists in early form, and single facts can be edited directly in the weights without retraining. Lifting a capability out of one model and fitting it into another is still a vision: it waits on circuits that can be cleanly separated.今天的模型像一个只能整体重新编译的二进制文件：改哪里都得重新训练。读懂内部，才可能用上软件工程里习以为常的工具。在特征层面对比两个版本已经有了雏形；单条事实也已经可以直接在权重里修改，不必重新训练。至于把一个能力从一个模型里取出来、装进另一个模型，目前还是愿景：前提是电路能被干净地分开。

Switch views.切换视图。

A breakthrough would take us from “it seems to work” to “we know why it works”: able to fix it, steer it, audit it, learn from it, and in the end maintain it like software.突破会让我们从“它好像能用”走到“我们知道它为什么能用”：修得了它，调得了它，查得了它，学得了它，最终像软件一样维护它。

04

Where the field stands现状与局限

4.1

How far we have come.已经走了多远。

In six years the field went from naming single neurons in an image model to tracing the computation of production language models, and now to models that describe their own internal states.六年间，这个领域从给图像模型里的单个神经元命名，走到追踪生产级大模型的计算过程，再到让模型描述自己的内部状态。

Hover the timeline.悬停时间线。

4.2

Most of the map is still blank.地图的大部分仍是空白。

The best tools today explain only part of what a model computes. By the researchers’ own estimate, attribution graphs give satisfying insight for about a quarter of the prompts they try, and even then part of the computation remains unexplained “dark matter”.今天最好的工具也只能解释模型计算的一部分。按研究者自己的估计，归因图只对大约四分之一的提示词能给出令人满意的解释；即使在这些提示词上，仍有一部分计算是无法解释的“暗物质”。

4.3

The features are not unique.特征并不唯一。

A dictionary is a choice, not a discovery. Train it again, or make it larger, and concepts split differently and the boundaries move. We do not yet know which decomposition, if any, is the one the model actually uses.字典是一种选择，而不是一项发现。重新训练一次，或者把字典做大，概念就会以不同方式分裂，边界也会移动。模型真正使用的是哪一种拆法，甚至有没有这样一种拆法，我们还不知道。

Switch runs.切换训练结果。

4.4

Understanding does not scale yet.理解还无法规模化。

Even for a prompt of a few dozen words, reading the circuit takes a researcher a few hours. Models answer far more than people could ever read, so interpretation has to become automated before it can keep up.即使提示词只有几十个词，读懂它背后的电路也要研究员花上几个小时。模型回答的量远远超出人力所能阅读，解释工作必须自动化，才可能跟得上。

This is where the field stands: real tools, real findings, and most of the map still blank. Lens is here to help fill it in.这就是领域的现状：有了真正的工具，也有了真正的发现，但地图的大部分仍是空白。Lens 要做的，就是一起把它填上。
